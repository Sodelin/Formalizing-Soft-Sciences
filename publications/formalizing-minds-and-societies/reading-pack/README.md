# Formalizing Minds and Societies

This reading pack accompanies two stand-alone academic works: a research article and an expanded dissertation-style monograph. Both develop the strongest scientific uses of a 67-theorem Lean library, with explicit model scope, author–date citations, references, and appendices.

The monograph expands *How We Formalized Questions About Society*. It explains each lemma and counterexample, develops the mathematical arguments, locates the work across disciplines, and proposes a concrete AI-reasoning evaluation.

The complete manuscripts are also included as single Markdown files. Use the PDFs for fixed APA-style pagination and the EPUBs for reflowable reading. EPUB readers control font size and page layout; the citations, reference structure, and scholarly organization remain the same.

## Research files

- `references.ris` and `references.bib`: Zotero-importable source metadata and tags.
- `source-register.csv`: access depth and evidence role.
- `source-relations.csv`: conceptual links proposed by the synthesis.
- `theorem-inventory.csv`: atlas numbers, exact theorem names, and pinned source links.
- `search-log.md`: search scope, selected exact queries, and access outcomes.

The repository's checked models remain at their pinned source revision. The manuscripts add interpretation, synthesis, and proposed research; they do not change the Lean code.
