The immediate release is the main branch of [Formalizing Soft Sciences](https://github.com/Sodelin/Formalizing-Soft-Sciences). It contains readable Markdown reports, the Lean source, an exact theorem inventory, citations, and the verification record. The original paper, report, and downloadable documents remain linked from the root. GitHub availability is not peer review.

## Steps toward a scholarly contribution

1. Recover the previous mathematics-of-psychology corpus and preserve its provenance. Identify overlap before proposing a new methods contribution.
2. Request separate reviews of mathematical meaning and social-scientific interpretation. A useful review asks whether the theorem states the intended claim, whether assumptions are plausible, and which existing work already answers it.
3. Choose one substantive, bounded target from the problem register. Develop it beyond the current elementary examples and compare it with the strongest relevant results.
4. Prepare a methods manuscript with a source-to-theorem mapping, assumption sensitivity, reproducible build, and a realistic example. Describe AI assistance and human review accurately.
5. Prepare a versioned archive and consider a suitable preprint or journal route once authorship, contribution, and venue fit are settled. The earlier [publication record](../solidarity-at-scale/publication/README.md) links official archiving and submission guidance.

No archival DOI, preprint submission, journal submission, or researcher outreach was created by this expansion. The exact publishable claim today is a checked educational and research scaffold with documented limits. Stronger publication claims require the stronger work described above.
