## Questions for a research notebook

What is the construct? Which observations are actually available? Which distinct mechanisms remain compatible with them? What assumptions eliminate those alternatives? Which assumptions can be tested, bounded, or challenged? What conclusion survives when an assumption changes?

Record the answer before labeling a statement a law, an identified causal effect, or an open problem.
