# Review protocol and next evidence pass

This records the method actually used for version 0.1 and specifies a future improvement. It is not retrospective preregistration. The initial search used purposive web discovery, original publisher or author records, PubMed metadata, and historical repositories. Search providers were web search and PubMed retrieval; no claim is made to have searched every bibliographic database.

The question is whether demographic similarity, perceived familiarity, symbolic comprehension, aligned incentives, and institutional reliability explain cooperation differently as the relevant population grows. Scale means direct ties, reachable network size, organizational membership, or jurisdictional population; these are distinct quantities. Outcomes include costly contribution, reliability, inclusion, distribution of gains, voice, durability, and harm to outsiders.

Include a source when it discriminates a mechanism, provides a credible outcome comparison, establishes what a historical actor publicly argued, or articulates an evaluative premise. Exclude general commentary used as substitute for a retrievable original study, unsupported biological essentialism, and causal claims inferred solely from a speech. Preserve relevant null findings and contradictory cases. Record inaccessible but relevant materials as leads rather than assessed evidence.

The initial register contains 35 sources selected for relevance, not 35 studies from a systematic screening funnel. Seven quantitative studies or reviews receive structured extraction; other records support conceptual or historical analysis. Search-log.json contains the retained execution-pass queries and opens. Earlier conversational leads were not reconstructed into fictitious screening counts. One broad engine-1 batch returned noisy results; primary pages and targeted follow-up searches were used instead. Source-specific limitations record failed PDF retrieval, a timeout, subscription limits, and abstract-only access.

For the next pass, freeze eligibility and a search cutoff before retrieval. Search PsycINFO, Sociological Abstracts, EconLit, a multidisciplinary citation index, and an open scholarly index where access permits. Report which were actually available. Use query families combining cooperation/solidarity/collective action with homophily, identity complexity, intergroup contact, cultural markers, transference, diversity, institutions, and coalition. Add citation chasing from both supporting and challenging papers. Extend beyond English through relevant regional scholarship and translation review.

Two independent reviewers should screen and extract decisive claims, resolving differences in a recorded log. Record participant and cluster counts, exclusions, assignment, compliance, attrition by arm, outcomes, follow-up, estimands, uncertainty, multiplicity, preregistration, funding, and contextual constraints. Do not classify a natural attribute as randomized because participants were randomly paired. For observational and instrumental-variable studies, specify selection processes, adjustment choices, exclusion restrictions, and plausible violations.

For history, identify the version, date, audience, political setting, editorial mediation, and differences from earlier and later texts. Use correspondence to investigate motive where available; do not infer hidden intent from an inconvenient outcome. Outcome comparison needs a separate case-selection protocol including failures, repression, external aid, and feasible alternatives.

Pool effects only after defining a coherent outcome, intervention, population, and dependence structure. A future meta-analysis would specify effect conversion, multilevel dependence or robust variance, heterogeneity estimation, and selection sensitivity. This version computes none of those statistics. Keep mathematical entailments, causal estimates, historical interpretation, and normative judgments in separate claim types.

Decision rule: revise a recommendation when stronger contrary evidence changes the balance for the specified outcome, or when an arrangement violates its stated rights and distribution criteria. Citation volume alone is not a stopping or success rule.
