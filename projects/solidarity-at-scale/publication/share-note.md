# Shareable project description

The first version of **Solidarity at Scale** is public in [Formalizing Soft Sciences](https://github.com/Sodelin/Formalizing-Soft-Sciences). It explores how familiarity, cultural signs, shared interests, and institutions might support cooperation beyond small groups. The repository includes a paper, decision report, evidence tables, and 16 checked Lean theorems. The mathematics clarifies small models; it does not prove a political ideology or a psychological law. The [reader guide](../formal/what-lean-proves.md) explains each proof, prior work, and the next research steps. The substantial earlier psychology corpus is still being located for import. Comments on the evidence, assumptions, and historical interpretation are welcome.

This text is prepared for sharing. No direct outreach, mailing, or social-media posting has been performed.
