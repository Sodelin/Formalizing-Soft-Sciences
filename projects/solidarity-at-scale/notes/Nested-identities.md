# Nested identities

Geographic memberships can nest while professions, faiths, and interests cross boundaries. [[S28]] and [[S06]] treat perceived overlap as a psychological construct. The formal membership examples demonstrate logical compatibility only.

Distinguish administrative containment, identification, network reach, and invariance across scale. None can stand in for all the others. [[S01]] and [[S02]] motivate separating direct relationships from an organization's total membership.
