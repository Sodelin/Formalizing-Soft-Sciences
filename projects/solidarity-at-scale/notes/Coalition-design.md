# Coalition design

Evaluate contribution, retention, benefit distribution, voice, dissent, and harm to outsiders. Shared projects can help without ensuring wider transfer [[S08]]; output delivery can improve without broader institutional change [[S11]].

Protected group voice with cross-group collaboration is a proposed synthesis under equal-rights and non-domination values. [[S19]] provides a conceptual compatibility case. [[S13]] motivates matching tasks to governance levels. Neither makes the combined proposal a validated universal program.
