# Solidarity at scale

Question: which combinations of familiarity, symbolic competence, shared interests, and reliable institutions support cooperation beyond personal acquaintance?

Working answer: a conditional mechanism account is better supported than a universal demographic-homogeneity requirement. Political recommendations also depend on explicit values and remain less certain than the component findings.
