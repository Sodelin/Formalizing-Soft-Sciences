# Model assumptions

The path graph separates local degree from indirect reach. The donation game separates cooperation incentives from labels. Its c ≤ s threshold assumes certain sanctions imposed by an external institution with no modeled cost or legitimacy problem.

A counterexample refutes a universal implication under the stated definitions. It supplies no estimate of real-world prevalence. Keep proof status separate from construct validity and evidence quality. See formal/specification.md and formal/verification-report.md.
