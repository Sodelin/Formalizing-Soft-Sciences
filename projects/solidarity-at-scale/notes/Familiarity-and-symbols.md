# Familiarity and symbols

[[S05]] supplies a relational-self hypothesis; [[S04]] supplies a marker-information mechanism. Measure perceived resemblance, relationship valence, comprehension, endorsement, and expected reciprocity separately. A cultural reference may be understood without being endorsed. A familiar person may evoke distrust rather than safety.

The empirical proposal separates experimental cultural learning from material incentives and guarantees. A later study can manipulate resemblance using nonidentifying descriptors. Neither pathway is yet established as a sufficient explanation of political coalitions.
