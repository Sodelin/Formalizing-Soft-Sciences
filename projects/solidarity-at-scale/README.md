# Solidarity at scale

An initial research project on how personal relationships, cultural signs, shared interests, and institutions support cooperation across groups and levels of organization. Prepared from Nolan Downard’s research questions; research assistance and elementary formal development by Codex. Version 0.1, 27 September 2026 Pacific time.

The evidence favors a conditional account of cooperation. Under equal-rights, non-domination, and material-inclusion goals, the working recommendation combines protected group voice with practical cross-group collaboration and accountable representation. Evidence for that entire political package is limited; no universal ideological winner is claimed.

Start with the [decision report](report/research-report.md), then the [manuscript](paper/manuscript.md). Rendered PDF and editable DOCX versions are in [outputs](outputs). The report separates process integrity in section 11 from inference robustness in section 12.

For an introduction to the mathematics, read [What Lean proves](formal/what-lean-proves.md), also available as a [PDF](outputs/what-lean-proves.pdf) and [editable document](outputs/what-lean-proves.docx). It covers all 16 theorems, their psychological interpretation, a proposed Durkheim model, prior formalization work, and dissemination. The earlier psychology corpus is still [pending recovery and import](../../psychology/README.md).
